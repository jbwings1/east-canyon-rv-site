Plan your stay at East Canyon

East Canyon Resort offers RV camping with full hookups, furnished condominium rentals, and group sites for reunions and special gatherings – all on private land in the Morgan canyon country.

Choose what fits your trip and book online. When a site or unit is open for your dates, your reservation is confirmed immediately and a confirmation email is sent to you. Pick your dates below to see which RV sites are open on the map.

How it works

  1. Choose condo, family reunion, or RV and pick your dates.
  2. Select an open unit on the map that matches your reservation type.
  3. Book online – if it is available, your reservation is confirmed right away.
  4. Check your email for your confirmation and arrival details.
